This week’s pulse

The Chinese-language ecosystem this week skews heavily toward platform-native tooling: scrapers, downloaders, and automation layers built around Bilibili, Douyin, and A-share markets that Western trackers almost never surface. What stands out is how much engineering effort goes into working around closed-platform constraints — danmaku extraction, multi-account check-in bots, gamepad-first clients for consoles — a whole genre of pragmatic hacking that Silicon Valley discourse tends to ignore. The clear pick of the week is LangBot, a genuinely production-grade platform that wires LLMs into virtually every messaging app on the planet, including the Chinese ones (WeChat, DingTalk, Lark, QQ) that Western chatbot frameworks treat as afterthoughts. If you only look at one repo, make it that one.

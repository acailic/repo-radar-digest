Latest — 2026-W47 (Nov 16 – Nov 22, 2026)

Two threads dominate this roundup: multi-agent autonomy that actually ships, and tooling built for audiences the English-speaking scene tends to ignore. N1nEmAn/sapper-v2 is the standout — a model-agnostic orchestration harness whose commander-general-soldier "pentest legion" took first place in a Tencent attack-defense challenge fully unattended, burning 1B tokens to beat a runner-up spending 4.5B, and it hints at where long-horizon cooperative agents go next. Around it sit quieter gems the West is largely sleeping on: Fudan's MOSS-Image bringing disciplined text rendering and layout control to an open 5B DiT, deepopen's non-autoregressive intent engine treating LLMs as probability-emitting classifiers rather than chatterboxes, and a thriving fringe of Chinese-language tooling from real-time visual novel translation to light-novel machine-translation pipelines. If you only clone one thing, make it sapper-v2.
